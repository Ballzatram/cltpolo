What counts as a documented match?

Every required check must pass: 20+ parcel acres, a documented flat usable footprint, an arena-first layout, a separate grass format, southern I-77 access, and a recorded route of 45 minutes or less from Uptown Charlotte. A training grass area is not labeled as a full-size field.

Listing claims are not survey evidence. The app checks an analyst-recorded source, status and review date; it does not certify engineering, drainage or permitted use. Confirm availability with the broker.

Research weights: flatness 40, arena 25, grass 15, access/drive 10, acreage 5, optional expansion 5. Price, votes, extra acreage and historic scores never override failed requirements. These points are not an appraisal.
