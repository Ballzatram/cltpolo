What counts as a documented match?

All required checks must pass: 20+ parcel acres, a documented flat usable footprint, an arena-first layout, a separate grass format, southern I-77 access, and a recorded route of 45 minutes or less from Uptown Charlotte. A grass training area is not labeled as a full-size field.

Listing claims are not survey evidence. The app checks that an analyst recorded a source, status and review date; it does not independently certify a survey, zoning, drainage or engineering design. Confirm current availability with the broker.

Research-priority weights: flatness 40, arena 25, grass 15, access/drive 10, acreage 5, optional expansion 5. Price, extra acreage, votes and historic scores cannot override a failed requirement. An over-limit legacy drive estimate stays outside the current screen until a better route check resolves it.
