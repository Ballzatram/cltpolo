Current acquisition brief

Charlotte toward Columbia

Stay along I-77, within approximately 45 minutes of Uptown Charlotte. Columbia defines the direction, not an exemption from the drive limit. No minimum drive time.
Flatness is a gate, not a trade-off. Cheap land, extra acreage, old scores, and votes cannot compensate for unsuitable terrain. A listing saying “flat” is a lead, not verification. Confirm a contiguous usable footprint and a safe arena/grass layout; parcel acreage alone does not establish either.

Required

At least 20 acres, flat usable polo land, an arena-first layout with a separately assessed grass format, southern I-77 access, and a route within 45 minutes.

Nice to have

Usable expansion land or a documented neighboring purchase opportunity. More acreage is not automatically more usable land.

Before a site visit

Review parcel contours and boundaries, drainage/floodplain, access, zoning and utilities. Check the drive at the intended departure time. Distinguish a training grass area from a full-field layout.

How fit and verification work

Required checks must all pass before a property is labeled “Meets documented brief.” Flat terrain contributes 40 points, arena layout 25, grass format 15, documented access/drive 10, minimum acreage 5, and optional expansion 5. These are transparent research-priority points, not an appraisal, engineering approval, or probability of success. Legacy scores and votes do not alter eligibility.

A documented check requires a status, a source link and a valid review date. Drive evidence must use Uptown Charlotte. Legacy drive figures are explicitly unverified estimates; estimates above 45 minutes remain outside the current screen until a better route check establishes otherwise. A documented grass training format does not claim a full-size field.
