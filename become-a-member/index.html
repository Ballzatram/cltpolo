A Club Built Around Participation

Charlotte Polo Club is still in an early chapter, which makes membership less about a finished package and more about helping shape what comes next. We are building a local base for players, supporters, sponsors, and friends of the sport who want to see polo take root in Charlotte again.

Early Member Interest

Membership details will continue to evolve as the club grows. For now, the best first step is to reach out, tell us how you would like to participate, and join the conversation around practices, events, support, and future club access.

Membership Paths

Final membership levels, benefits, and pricing are in development as the club grows.

Playing

Player Membership

$2,000 annual

For riders who want to practice, improve, and help build a consistent playing community in the Charlotte region.

  • Practice and chukker interest
  • Player updates and scheduling
  • Early involvement in club development

Field-Side

Event Membership

$1,600 annual

For supporters who want a closer connection to match days, tailgates, exhibitions, and future club gatherings.

  • Event and tailgate interest
  • Member communications
  • Priority awareness as plans develop

Social

Social Membership

$1,000 annual

For people who love the atmosphere of polo and want to connect with the club through events, hospitality, and community.

  • Social events and mixers
  • Club news and invitations
  • A path into the polo community
