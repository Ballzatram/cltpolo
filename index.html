Building Something That Lasts

Charlotte Polo Club is being rebuilt with long-term sustainability in mind. Our aim is not simply to host matches, but to create a durable foundation for polo in the Charlotte region through events, education, participation, and thoughtful growth. The goal is to build a club structure that respects the sport, supports its future, and gives it a lasting place here again.

A Revival with Purpose

Charlotte Polo Club is rooted in revival. The region once had an active polo presence, but despite later efforts to bring the sport back, nothing built lasting momentum. We are a group of players and enthusiasts working to change that by rebuilding a club with long-term purpose. Our goal is not only to honor Charlotte’s polo history, but to grow the sport in a way that is credible, sustainable, and built to endure.

Sport

Build visibility and access around a historic equestrian sport.

Community

Create events, learning pathways, and a stronger local polo network.

Sustainability

Focus on responsible growth that can last beyond a single season.
