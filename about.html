About Charlotte Polo Club

Reintroducing polo to Charlotte with a long-term vision for sport, culture, and sustainable growth.

History

Polo and the Charlotte Region

Polo has long occupied a unique place in the equestrian world, combining athleticism, horsemanship, and tradition. While Charlotte and the surrounding region have deep ties to equestrian culture, polo has not maintained the kind of durable local presence that its landscape and community could support. Charlotte Polo Club exists to help change that.

Why It Matters

Why Revival Matters

Charlotte Polo Club is not simply about bringing back a sport for nostalgia’s sake. It is about creating a modern and sustainable structure around polo in a region that can support meaningful participation, public engagement, and long-term development. The goal is to build something that lasts.

Long-Term Vision

Building for the Long Term

Sport

Encourage learning, participation, and regional competition through a credible polo platform rooted in Charlotte.

Education

Create accessible pathways for newcomers to understand and enter the sport with confidence.

Events

Build a culture around matches, hospitality, and public-facing experiences that make polo visible again.

Stewardship

Support responsible growth rooted in land, horses, and enduring community value.

The Future

Looking Ahead

The long-term ambition for Charlotte Polo Club is to help establish a lasting home for the sport in the region: one that welcomes spectators, supports new riders, creates meaningful partnerships, and contributes to Charlotte’s broader sporting and cultural landscape.
